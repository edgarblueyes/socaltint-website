Who We Are

Experts In Automotive Tinting

SoCal Tint has been in business since 1995, providing professional tinting for auto, home and commercial windows. Your satisfaction is our goal — that's why we strive to provide the best there is in the industry.

We specialize in flat-glass window tinting for businesses and homes, safety & security films, vehicle wraps and paint protection film. We're an XPEL Flagship dealer and an authorized Clearplex dealer.

  • Automotive, Residential & Commercial
  • PPF, Ceramic Coating & Custom Wraps
  • Tesla builds & custom wraps
