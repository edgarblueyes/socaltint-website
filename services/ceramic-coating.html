Choose Your Level

Ceramic Coating Packages

From a quick protective layer to full multi-stage paint correction — a package for every vehicle and budget. All can be combined with Clear Bra PPF and car wraps.

Bronze

Exterior foam wash. Windows and mirrors cleaned. Application of ceramic coating. Can be combined with Clear Bra PPF & car wraps.

Silver

Deep exterior cleaning & foam wash. All paintwork and glass decontaminated with Iron-X and clay-bar treatment. Ceramic coating applied; tires & trim conditioned; glass cleaned.

Gold Paint Correction

Deep cleaning, bug/tar/sap removal, Iron-X & clay-bar decontamination, plus a 1-stage paint enhancement to remove swirls and restore a deep gloss. Ceramic coating applied; tires & trim conditioned.

Platinum Top Tier

Everything in Gold, plus a full 2-stage paint correction to remove deeper imperfections and restore a mirror-like gloss. Ceramic coating applied; tires & trim conditioned; glass cleaned.

Lock In That Shine

Ask about combining ceramic coating with PPF for the ultimate protection package.
