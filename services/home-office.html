VISION Solutions

A Film For Every Need

Solar

Solar energy rejection plus security features — Clear View Plus, Clear View, Daylight, Crystal Clear, Evening View, One Way, Bronze & Silver.

Safety

A virtually clear layer of strong PET with 99% UV filtration — secures glass in the event of a breakage. 4 Mil & 7 Mil.

Security

Deter unauthorized entry and burglary. Available in 8 Mil, 12 Mil and 15 Mil thicknesses.

Anti-Graffiti

Protects glass vandalized by graffiti, scratching or acid. Clear 4 & 6 Mil for interior or exterior.

Exterior

Exterior reflectivity for privacy and solar-heat blocking — comfort and energy savings. Silver 20% VLT.

Decorative

Decorative & Privacy Film

Custom Looks, A Fraction Of The Cost

Decorative window film is a beautiful way to protect your privacy — the look of expensive glazed, frosted, etched glass for far less. We carry custom colors, textures and patterns for interior glass and the inside of exterior windows, residential or commercial, with a 5-year warranty.

Add privacy, enhance design, increase safety, reject UV and cut glare. We offer frost bands, custom logos cut into frost, logos printed onto frost, Solyx films, vinyl overlays — plus custom vinyl lettering and storefront graphics. Call for a free on-site consultation and free film samples.

  • White Frost — semi-transparent privacy where sunlight is still desired.
  • Whiteout & Blackout — opaque films for total privacy and view elimination.
  • View Control (Lumisty) — transparent from one angle, translucent from another.
  • Transparent Colored & Patterned Films — hundreds of designs and custom colors.
  • Custom Vinyl Lettering & Graphics — storefront graphics, logos and borders.
