The XPEL Lineup

Revolutionary Products

From paint protection to window tint and architectural film, XPEL materials are on the cutting edge of technology and quality.

ULTIMATE PLUS PPF

Self-healing paint protection film that shields high-impact areas — hood, bumper, fenders, mirrors — from rocks, bugs, sand, salt and keys. Gloss or matte, professionally cut for a near-invisible finish.

STEALTH SATIN PPF

As smooth as satin. Whether your vehicle is matte, magno, frozen or frosted, self-healing STEALTH keeps it pristine — or convert your gloss paint to a whole new satin look.

PRIME WINDOW TINT

Tint that looks cool and feels cool — outperforming other films to keep you comfortable and protected, all backed by the industry's only lifetime transferable warranty.

FUSION PLUS CERAMIC

A ceramic coating built to perform with PPF and painted surfaces — unrivaled gloss, superior hydrophobic protection and improved scratch resistance in a single application.
